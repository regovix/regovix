Paper forms that never get filed. Spreadsheets nobody trusts. Procedures last updated three years ago. Incidents that never get closed out. Sound familiar?

Paper forms that fail audits

Primary duty to manage risks

Requires elimination or minimisation of risks so far as is reasonably practicable. Documented risk assessments are the evidence of compliance.

Do I need all 12 apps or can I start with one?
Start with one app — whichever solves your most urgent compliance problem right now. RiskMatrix at $29/month is the fastest to try. Add more apps as you need them, or bundle all 12 at $749/month and save versus individual pricing.
Is Regovix built for Australian WHS law or adapted from overseas software?
Built specifically for Australian WHS law. Every app references the WHS Act 2011, WHS Regulation 2017, ADG Code 9th Edition, HVNL, and relevant AS/NZS standards — not adapted from US or UK products.
How long does it take to get set up?
Most customers are using their first app within one week. We personally configure your account, set up your sites and users, and onboard your team at no charge. You focus on the work — we handle the setup.
Can HSEQ consultants manage multiple clients from one account?
Yes. The Consultant plan lets you manage up to 15 client companies from a single dashboard with white-label PDF output under your own branding. It is the most cost-effective way for consultants to deliver compliance services at scale.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation. You can export all records, documents, and reports as PDF or CSV at any time before or after cancellation. Your data is yours — always.
